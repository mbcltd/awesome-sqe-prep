# Awesome SQE Prep [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of resources for the Solicitors Qualifying Examination (SQE) — the route to qualifying as a solicitor in England and Wales.

The SQE is administered by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA) and delivered by Kaplan. This list focuses on **SQE1** — the two Functioning Legal Knowledge papers (FLK1 and FLK2), each 180 single-best-answer MCQs — because that is the multiple-choice stage where practice questions matter most. SQE2 (practical skills) is covered briefly for awareness, but is not MCQ-based and is out of scope for the bulk of this list.

Entries cover the official specification and sample questions, free and paid practice tools, prep providers, books, the regulatory standards behind the syllabus, and where candidates discuss the qualification.

## Official Resources

- [Kaplan Assessments — SQE](https://kaplanassessments.com/) — Kaplan is the SRA-appointed assessment organisation that designs and delivers the SQE. Useful background on how the assessment is produced.
- [LawCAB — SQE applications](https://www.lawcabs.ac.uk/sqe) — The Central Applications Board, used to apply to SQE prep courses at participating institutions.
- [SQE Assessment Regulations](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/standards-regulations/sqe-assessment-regulations/) — The SRA rules governing eligibility, conduct, results, and resits.
- [SQE Reports & Statistics](https://sqe.sra.org.uk/sqe-results/reports) — Sitting-by-sitting statistical reports with pass rates, score distributions, and diversity data published by the SRA after each sitting.
- [SQE Sample Questions (SQE1)](https://sqe.sra.org.uk/assessments/sqe1-assessments/sqe1-sample-questions) — Over 200 official SRA sample questions across FLK1 and FLK2, free to access without registration.
- [SQE1 Assessment Specification](https://sqe.sra.org.uk/assessments/sqe1-assessments/sqe1-specification) — The official syllabus for FLK1 and FLK2: subject areas, how legal authorities are tested, and how ethics is woven through.
- [SRA — Solicitors Qualifying Examination](https://www.sra.org.uk/become-solicitor/sqe/) — The regulator's overview of the SQE route to qualification.
- [The SQE Website (sqe.sra.org.uk)](https://sqe.sra.org.uk/) — Official candidate portal: register, book assessments, access results, and read the current specification.

## Free Practice & Study Tools

- [BARBRI SQE Edge](https://www.barbri.com/sqe/sqe-edge) — Free SQE1 MCQs from BARBRI's question bank, with explanations.
- [BPP SQE Sample Resources](https://www.bpp.com/law/sqe/sqe-resources) — Free sample questions and a 100-question mock from BPP University.
- [Go SQE1](https://gosqe.com/en) — Free practice questions covering FLK1 organised by subject and topic, with detailed explanations referencing statutes and case law. Freemium model with paid access to the full FLK1 and FLK2 bank. (Maintained by the publisher of this list.)
- [Law Drills](https://www.lawdrills.com/) — Adaptive SQE1 practice platform with a free tier of practice questions and mini-mocks; paid subscription unlocks the full bank.
- [QLTS School — Free SQE Sample Material](https://www.qlts.co.uk/resources/sqe-sample-material/) — Two free 50-question mock tests (one for FLK1, one for FLK2) with full answer explanations.
- [UK SQE Course — Free SQE1 Sample Questions](https://uksqecourse.com/samples) — Free SQE1 single-best-answer questions with full explanations: 10 without an account, 25 with a free account. Paid plans unlock the full question bank.

## Paid Study Providers

- [BARBRI SQE Prep](https://www.barbri.com/sqe) — Online SQE1 and SQE2 courses with personal study plans, structured workbooks, and a pass guarantee. Widely used by international candidates dual-qualifying.
- [BPP University SQE Courses](https://www.bpp.com/law/sqe/courses) — SQE1, SQE2, combined diploma, and LLM-route options with online and in-person teaching.
- [FQPS Academy](https://fqps.co.uk/) — Lower-cost subscription-based SQE prep with AI-assisted study plans and tutor support.
- [The College of Legal Practice](https://www.collegalpractice.com/) — Virtual law school offering SQE1 and SQE2 prep courses and an LLM in Legal Practice; positioned at the lower end of the price range.
- [University of Law — SQE Courses](https://www.law.ac.uk/study/postgraduate/sqe/courses/) — SQE1 and SQE2 preparation, including the ULawSQE revision app. Available full-time, part-time, on-campus, and online.

## Books

- *Revise SQE Business Law and Practice* by Benjamin Jones (Fink Publishing) — FLK1 revision guide covering business structures, financing, taxation, and insolvency. ISBN: 9781914213144.
- *Revise SQE Constitutional and Administrative Law and EU Law* by Richard Clements (Fink Publishing) — FLK1 revision guide for the Legal System subject area. ISBN: 9781917183260.
- *Revise SQE Wills and the Administration of Estates* by Sheila Hamilton Macdonald (Fink Publishing) — FLK2 revision guide on wills, probate, and estate administration. ISBN: 9781914213892.
- *SQE1 — Wills and the Administration of Estates* by Jacqueline Kempton and Gill Williams (College of Legal Practice / Wildy) — Practitioner-style coverage of the FLK2 wills syllabus. ISBN: 9781914219085.

## Background Reading

- [SRA Code of Conduct for Solicitors](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/standards-regulations/code-conduct-solicitors/) — The behavioural standards expected of qualified solicitors. Ethics and professional conduct are woven through every SQE1 paper.
- [SRA Principles](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/standards-regulations/principles/) — The seven overarching ethical principles that underpin the Code of Conduct and the SQE ethics questions.
- [SRA Standards and Regulations](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/standards-regulations/) — The full regulatory framework that replaced the SRA Handbook in 2019.
- [SRA Statement of Solicitor Competence](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/resources/continuing-competence/competence-statement/) — The competence framework that the SQE assessments are built against.
- [Solicitors Qualifying Examination — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solicitors_Qualifying_Examination) — Neutral overview of the SQE, its history, costs, and the transition from the LPC and QLTS routes.

## SQE2 Awareness

SQE2 tests practical legal skills — client interviewing, advocacy, legal writing, drafting, legal research, and case analysis — through oral and written assessments. It is not MCQ-based, so this list does not curate SQE2 practice material in depth. The links below are starting points for candidates who have passed SQE1 and are planning SQE2 prep.

- [SQE2 Assessment Specification](https://sqe.sra.org.uk/assessments/sqe2-assessments/sqe2-specification) — Official SRA specification covering the six skills, five practice areas, and marking approach.
- [SQE2 Sample Questions](https://sqe.sra.org.uk/assessments/sqe2-assessments/sqe2-sample-questions) — Free official sample tasks across written and oral assessments.
